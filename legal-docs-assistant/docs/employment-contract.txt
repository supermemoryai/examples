EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT (TEMPLATE — EXAMPLE ONLY)

This Employment Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between [COMPANY],
a Delaware corporation (the "Company"), and [EMPLOYEE NAME] (the
"Employee") effective as of [START DATE].

1. POSITION AND DUTIES
   The Employee will serve as [TITLE], reporting to [MANAGER]. The
   Employee will devote their full business time and attention to the
   Company and will not engage in any other employment without the prior
   written consent of the Company.

2. COMPENSATION
   The Employee will receive an annual base salary of $[AMOUNT], paid in
   accordance with the Company's standard payroll practices, and is
   eligible for an annual performance bonus targeted at [BONUS]% of base
   salary, subject to Company and individual performance.

3. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ASSIGNMENT
   The Employee assigns to the Company all right, title, and interest in
   any inventions, works of authorship, or other intellectual property
   created within the scope of employment or using Company resources.
   The Employee will execute any documents reasonably required to
   perfect such assignment. This assignment does not apply to inventions
   developed entirely on the Employee's own time without Company
   resources that do not relate to the Company's business, to the extent
   required by applicable state law.

4. NON-COMPETE AND NON-SOLICITATION
   For a period of twelve (12) months following termination of
   employment, the Employee will not, within any geographic area in
   which the Company actively does business, directly or indirectly
   engage in any business that competes with the Company. For the same
   twelve-month period, the Employee will not solicit any Company
   employee or customer with whom the Employee had material contact.
   Enforceability of this Section 4 is subject to applicable state law.

5. CONFIDENTIALITY
   The Employee will hold all Company confidential information in strict
   confidence during and after employment and will return all Company
   property and materials upon termination.

6. TERMINATION
   Employment is at-will and may be terminated by either Party at any
   time, with or without cause and with or without notice. Upon
   termination for any reason, the Employee is entitled to accrued but
   unpaid base salary and any vested benefits. If terminated by the
   Company without cause, the Employee is entitled to severance equal
   to three (3) months of base salary, subject to execution of a release
   of claims.

7. GOVERNING LAW
   This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.
