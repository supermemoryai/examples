SAAS SERVICE AGREEMENT (TEMPLATE — EXAMPLE ONLY)

This Service Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between [PROVIDER],
a Delaware corporation ("Provider"), and [CUSTOMER] ("Customer") as of
[EFFECTIVE DATE].

1. SERVICES
   Provider will make its hosted software-as-a-service platform (the
   "Service") available to Customer pursuant to the order form executed by
   the Parties. Customer receives a non-exclusive, non-transferable right
   to access and use the Service for its internal business purposes during
   the Term.

2. FEES AND PAYMENT
   Customer will pay the fees set forth in the order form. Invoices are
   due net thirty (30) days. Late amounts accrue interest at 1.5% per
   month or the maximum rate permitted by law, whichever is lower.

3. SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT
   Provider will use commercially reasonable efforts to maintain monthly
   uptime of at least 99.9%, excluding scheduled maintenance windows
   announced at least 48 hours in advance. If uptime falls below 99.9%
   in a calendar month, Customer is entitled to a service credit equal to
   10% of that month's fees; below 99.0%, the credit is 25%. Service
   credits are Customer's sole and exclusive remedy for SLA failures.

4. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
   EXCEPT FOR BREACHES OF CONFIDENTIALITY OR INDEMNIFICATION OBLIGATIONS,
   EACH PARTY'S AGGREGATE LIABILITY UNDER THIS AGREEMENT SHALL NOT EXCEED
   THE FEES PAID BY CUSTOMER IN THE TWELVE (12) MONTHS PRECEDING THE
   EVENT GIVING RISE TO THE CLAIM. NEITHER PARTY SHALL BE LIABLE FOR
   INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES.

5. TERM AND TERMINATION
   The initial term is twelve (12) months and renews automatically for
   successive twelve-month terms unless either Party gives written notice
   of non-renewal at least thirty (30) days before the end of the then-
   current term. Either Party may terminate for material breach if the
   breaching Party fails to cure within thirty (30) days after written
   notice. Provider may suspend the Service immediately for non-payment
   beyond fifteen (15) days past due.

6. DATA AND SECURITY
   Customer retains ownership of Customer Data. Provider will maintain
   industry-standard administrative, physical, and technical safeguards
   designed to protect Customer Data against unauthorized access.
